2.1 Initial Investment

2.2 Revenue Model
A. Outlet Revenue Distribution
The Gross or Top Line revenue generated from each operational outlet shall be distributed as follows:
Salon Operations – 50%
Franchise Owner  in Your Case it’s  to Salon Franchisee– 20%
Product & Marketing – 10%
Master Franchise Partner – 5%
Company – 15%
Note – Roi will be calculate on 70%  gross sale  share To Franchisee owner.
The City Franchise Partner shall be entitled to 20% of the Gross Sales (Top-Line Revenue) generated by the outlet. This revenue share shall be calculated before deduction of any operational expenses, overheads, taxes (where applicable), or other business costs. Under no circumstances shall the Franchise Partner be required to bear or contribute towards any operational losses, expenses, or deficits of the outlet.
B. No Liability Towards Property Rental
The Franchise Partner shall have no financial or legal liability in relation to the rental, lease, security   deposit, maintenance charges, or any other obligations arising from the property occupied by the outlet. Such responsibilities shall remain solely with the Company.
C. Additional Earnings
In addition to the above revenue share, the City Franchise Partner shall be entitled to:
₹15000/- (Rupees Fifteen Thousand Only) for every successful franchise sold within the allotted territory; and
2% Royalty on the sales turnover of all operational outlets functioning within the allotted territory, as per the Company’s approved accounting records.
2.3 Minimum Guaranteed Return (MG)
The Company shall provide the Franchise Partner with a Minimum Guaranteed Return (MG) equivalent to 5% of the Initial Investment, i.e., ₹/15000- per month, subject to the Franchise Partner’s compliance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement.
At the end of each calendar month, the Company shall calculate the total amount payable to the Franchise Partner, including revenue sharing, franchise commissions, royalties and all other earnings under this Agreement.
If the total actual earnings exceed the Minimum Guaranteed Return of ₹15000/- for that month, the Company shall pay the higher actual earnings.
If the total actual earnings are less than ₹15000/- for that month, the Company shall pay the Minimum Guaranteed Return of ₹15000/-.
Accordingly, the
Franchise Partner shall always be entitled to receive whichever amount is higher between:
The actual earnings accrued under this Agreement; or
The Minimum Guaranteed Return (MG) of 15000/- per month.
The Company shall release the payable amount on or before the 5th Working Day of the succeeding month, along with a detailed statement of accounts.
Minimum Guarantee / Commission Default
In the event the Franchise Partner does not receive the applicable Commission or Minimum Guarantee (MG) for two (2) consecutive months, the same shall be treated as a material breach of the Agreement by the Company. In such an event, the Franchise Partner shall have the right to terminate the Agreement and shall be entitled to an immediate refund of 90% of the Franchise Investment, subject to the terms of the Agreement.
2.4 Statements
The Company shall provide complete revenue statements on or before the 5th Working Day of every succeeding month.
A . No Adjustment of Operational Losses
Any operational losses, depreciation, business losses, or other liabilities incurred by the Company in operating the outlet shall not be adjusted, deducted, or recovered from the Franchise Partner's investment or refund amount. The Franchise Partner shall not be responsible for such liabilities.
B.Nominee
The Franchise Partner shall have the right to appoint one nominee for the outlet by submitting the prescribed nominee details and required documentation. Upon approval by the Company, the nominee shall be recognized for the purposes specified under the Agreement.

6. INVESTMENT REFUND
6.1 Refund Policy
Upon termination of this Agreement, the Company shall refund 90% of the Initial Investment, after adjustment of any outstanding dues, damages or liabilities, if any.
The deduction of 10% represents administrative expenses, documentation charges, onboarding, branding, training and other initial operational expenses already incurred by the Company.
The refund shall be processed within 30 (Thirty) Working Days from completion of settlement and handover formalities.
The Franchise Partner invests in at a franchise fee of ₹3,10,000 per unit, if the total ROI Cross 3X means 9,30,000/- (Rupees Twelve Lakhs Forty Thousand Only), exclusive of applicable GST.
In the event that the Franchise Agreement is terminated or mutually closed before the expiry of the agreed tenure, the Company shall refund 50% of the original investment amount made by the Franchise Partner.
7. TERM & TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for Three (3) Years from the Effective Date.
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving Thirty (30) Days’ Written Notice upon material breach which remains uncured during the notice period.
Upon termination:
Brand usage rights shall immediately cease.
Company property shall be returned.
Accounts shall be reconciled.
Confidentiality obligations shall survive.
7.1 Right of Franchise Partner
The Franchise Partner may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 (Thirty) days' written notice if the Company commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to rectify such breach within the notice period.
7.2 Temporary Closure of Salon
In the event the Salon is temporarily closed due to reasons solely attributable to the Company, the Company shall make reasonable efforts to provide an alternate operational location within 90 days.
During such period, the Minimum Guaranteed Return shall continue only if the Franchise Partner is not responsible for the closure and has complied with this Agreement.
If the closure is due to Force Majeure, Government restrictions, landlord disputes not attributable to the Company, or any default of the Franchise Partner, the Company shall not be liable to continue the Minimum Guaranteed Return during such period.
7.5 – Renewal
This Agreement may be renewed for a further period of Three (3) Years upon mutual written consent of both Parties, subject to satisfactory performance and compliance with the terms of this Agreement.
Any renewal fee, if applicable, shall be mutually agreed in writing before renewal.
The Minimum Guaranteed Return and other commercial terms applicable after renewal shall be governed by the renewal agreement executed between the Parties.
Upon successful completion of the initial contract period, the Franchise Partner shall have the first right to renew the franchise for the same outlet by paying a renewal fee of ₹20,000 (Rupees Twenty Thousand only). Upon payment of the renewal fee and execution of the renewal documents, the Agreement shall be extended for a further three (3) years on the prevailing renewal terms and conditions.
